# CLAIMS

What the Criticality Compass has shown, by class, on what data, and with what standing. One table; every evidence cell names a stored result and its sha256, and `tests/test_claims.py` checks that each one resolves.

Status words: **blind** = predictions stamped before the data were read, scored against a sealed truth. **development** = the rulebook, instrument or data was visible when it was built or read (synthetic maps included). **provisional** = scored, but on intervals or code known to be superseded; do not cite as a finding.

Evidence cells read root:path@sha256 (in code font). `cc:` is this repository; `toolset:` is the compass-toolset checkout (CC_TOOLSET, else ~/software/scratch/compass-toolset).

## Known limits

Read these before citing anything above.

- **Positive per-point readings outside the blind K4 LINE points are development readings only, and two kinds are false positives.** On the twelve v0.1 inputs the only positive per-point class is `x5n-bilayer` POINT. Inputs added after v0.1 read positive classes under rulebooks built with that family in view (rows above): `x9c-potts` FIRST-ORDER at q 6, 8, 10 and the XY low-phase LINE at T 0.5 to 0.85 agree with the truth; POINT at T_KT (`x2b-xy`, v05 to v07) and WALKING at T 0.6 (`x2b-xy` v05, `x2-xy-line-3` v04, v05) are false positives, refused or corrected by later rulebooks. The single positive family LINE (`b7-at-line-2`) rests on a boundary cell by 0.002. `cc:results/b7-at-line-2@v03-rules.json@25c836808bb25814a13883508c8d8afa0b5b15eab6d92dcdee827c443d348877`
- **Detector intervals under-cover for v08/v09 results.** Every result above produced under v08-rules or v09-rules (block or iid bootstrap on one chain) carries this; any statement above that depends on such an interval width inherits it. Detector v10 (interval method `chains`, m 10 independent chains, r 2) passes its coverage gate (toolset a43bb28): 0.976 to 0.983 in all 6 synthetic AR(1) cells against exact 0.9785, where the old method reproduced at 0.797. The gate is synthetic only; no input has yet been read under v10. `toolset:results/0930-ARM-M.txt@ef4b80514366fd857cb09543831d9ae1584c78de124c720d7da52bca63c60431`<br>`toolset:results/0937-P2-armM.txt@0c950187494a969d3970054eef9d6780545286a2bc4bf67b2e37b6e9995ec03c`
